Onboarding and email volume

The importer accepts up to 2,000 validated rows in a file of at most 2 MB. Accounts are processed separately from queued emails. Current application defaults are 10 sending attempts/minute and 400 per rolling 24 hours; these are conservative application settings, not a provider allowance. A 100-student import also needs capacity for staff onboarding, ticket notices, retries and other sender usage.

Before real recipients are enabled: replace developer/test addresses with approved institutional records; rotate previously exposed credentials; decide a safe initial-password/invitation process. Test intentional delivery to a small approved group first.

No data has been cleared or real mail enabled for this document. Before the agreed test-data cleanup, stop or cancel pending import/mail work so old jobs cannot recreate accounts or send stale messages.

03 / Network reachability and private media

Plan for calls across real student networks

Calls are one-to-one WebRTC sessions. Media normally travels between participants; the API handles coordination. When a direct connection fails, a TURN relay carries the media and adds bandwidth/cost. STUN alone does not cover every campus or mobile network. [R3]

ProvisionRequirement before enabling the feature publicly
TURN serviceApprove a managed relay or college-operated TURN host, bandwidth allowance, secure credential issuance and relevant UDP/TCP/TLS firewall rules. The developer must integrate authenticated, expiring credentials and prove a forced-relay call.
Network/device testsTest campus Wi-Fi to mobile data, two different external networks, restrictive campus networks, camera/microphone denial, reconnects and supported browsers. HTTPS and user permission are required for camera/microphone access. [R2, R3]
Recording storageApprove private object storage, region, budget, retention, deletion and backup policy. Store meeting/ownership metadata in PostgreSQL; use authenticated playback and resumable media uploads. Recordings must never use public file URLs.
Access and noticeStudent recording playback remains unavailable. Authorized ROs can view their permitted recordings. The Admin viewer remains deferred until requested and implemented. College approves recording notices/permissions and who may access or export files.
FallbackProvide an approved contact and rescheduling/in-person procedure if a call fails. If an institutional external meeting service is used as a temporary alternative, formally agree and test that workflow before advertising it.

Planning examples, not measured capacity

If both participants send 1 Mbps and all media is relayed, two simultaneous calls produce approximately 4 Mbps of TURN outbound traffic, or 1.8 GB/hour, excluding audio/protocol overhead. Actual rates and relay usage vary.

At 0.9 Mbps, video alone is approximately 203 MB per 30-minute recording. One hundred such recordings require approximately 20 GB before audio, metadata or backup copies. Higher bitrate/duration changes storage and cost.

Current implementation limit: videos remain in the RO browser until final upload; the server buffers an upload up to 256 MiB and stores bytes in PostgreSQL. Interrupted/long recordings can be lost or fail. Finish resumable private storage, or explicitly disable recording in code and verify the reduced pilot scope before launch.

PROPOSED PILOT100 INVITED STUDENTS

06 / Controlled rollout and acceptance

Use 100 students as a pilot cohort

100 registered participants does not mean 100 simultaneous users or calls. The proposed initial operating target is 20 concurrently active dashboards and no more than two simultaneous one-to-one calls, enforced operationally through bookings and supervision. These limits are proposals to test, not proven capacity or existing software-enforced limits.

StageActivity and gate to continue
Before invitationsClose all applicable S1–S9 gates. Test production staging, actual email recipients, permissions, restore and monitoring. If video/recording is excluded, verify its code/UI is disabled and communicate that scope.
Staff rehearsalUse approved test accounts to rehearse roster import, category routing, failed saves, schedule/reschedule, student-specific join links, minutes, escalation, resolution and feedback. Exercise the seven-day quota with test fixtures.
20 studentsInvite a representative first group with staff present. Check sign-in, email receipt, privacy, device usability and support response. Correct failures before adding participants.
50 studentsExpand after stable observation; test the agreed concurrent-dashboard target and two supervised calls on mixed networks. Measure database/API/relay and recording load where enabled.
100 studentsExpand only after the previous stage passes. Observe for a proposed 7–14 days, review incidents and user feedback, then obtain approval for the next cohort. General enrollment and public signup remain closed.

Proposed acceptance criteria — college to confirm

  • Privacy/data integrity: no unauthorized access; no lost or duplicated tickets, assignments, minutes or feedback in the tested workflows. Students receive a join prompt only for their own RO-started call.
  • Responsiveness: target p95 dashboard/action response below 2 seconds and less than 1% unexpected API failures during an agreed 30-minute workload. Measure on the chosen host; exclude intentional policy rejections.
  • Meetings: all planned network scenarios connect, including forced TURN; two concurrent calls remain usable for a representative session. If recording is enabled, test maximum intended duration, interruption, retry and authorized playback.
  • Delivery/recovery: approved test recipients receive intended messages; failures/backlogs are visible. Restore and restart tests preserve agreed data, queue state and access controls.

Pause immediately for data exposure, lost records, repeated wrong-recipient notices, sustained resource exhaustion or repeated call/recording failure. Stop invitations, inform the incident owner and use the approved fallback/rollback. Reopen only after the cause is fixed and rechecked.
